Vektaedge Technologies is a Kenyan technology company founded by a team of experienced software, DevOps, and mobile application engineers. The company brings together strong technical expertise and a shared passion for building innovative digital solutions that solve real-world problems.

Our mission is to empower businesses and communities through practical, reliable, and scalable technology. We focus on creating solutions that improve efficiency, enhance customer engagement, and support business growth.

One of our key products is Shop+, a loyalty and receipt management platform built for SMEs and small merchants. Shop+ enables businesses to reward their customers with points for every purchase, allowing customers to redeem rewards, track receipts, and enjoy enhanced benefits through flexible subscription-based packages.

Vektaedge Technologies is committed to innovation, excellence, and impact. We believe that technology should not only be advanced, but also accessible, useful, and capable of transforming everyday business operations.
